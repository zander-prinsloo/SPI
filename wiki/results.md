# Results

<!-- cg:auto:key-findings -->
The SPI produces country-level scores for each of the 22 dimensions and an overall index, enabling benchmarking of national statistical systems, identification of strengths and weaknesses, and tracking of progress over time.
<!-- cg:auto:end -->

<!-- cg:auto:tables -->
Key output files in `03_output_data/`:

- `SPI_data.csv` — final indicator values for each country
- `SPI_index.csv` — indicator values plus indices, including an overall country score
- `SPI_quantiles.csv` — quantile groupings for benchmarking
- Additional outputs include `SPI_databank_country_and_aggregates.csv` and related files
<!-- cg:auto:end -->

<!-- cg:auto:figures -->
The SPI is presented in dashboard format (see `02_programs/SPI_dashboard.PNG`) and as an index per country. The Shiny dashboard in `SPI_data_viz/` supports interactive dissemination of results.
